How Acadivue AI Works

1. Collect Academic Data

Attendance, Internal Marks, Assignments, Study Hours and Previous Semester Results are collected.

2. Analyze Data

The system analyzes student academic data using Python and Data Analytics.

3. Predict Performance

Machine Learning predicts future academic performance and identifies risk levels.

Students receive AI-powered recommendations to improve their academic performance.
